# Principia Desk logo — first exploration

[View the candidate](principia-desk-logo-concept-v1.png).

An open book with a subtle P in its central opening connects the broader learning
catalogue to the Principia name. Amber, aubergine, violet and an ivory serif wordmark
continue the desk's existing palette and editorial character. The violet point
suggests a learner above the book; the amber point suggests an initial idea.

This is a generated concept for review, not the installed app icon. Production
adoption would need an isolated mark, small-size inspection and desktop icon exports.
The original assets remain available. The refined raster is saved here so review
does not depend on a machine-specific generated-image directory.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. One initial generation and one
targeted refinement repaired the first output's broken transparency/edge artifacts.

## Initial prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: new logo concept for Principia Desk, a native desktop learning application spanning engineering, Linux/Bash and languages.
Primary request: create an original, polished logo exploration that expresses learning from first principles. The project has evolved beyond system-design roulette.
Composition: one clean horizontal logo lockup, generous whitespace, symbol on the left and the exact two-word name "Principia Desk" on the right. Large enough symbol to judge its small-icon silhouette.
Symbol: a distinctive, very simple geometric open-book mark constructed like a drafting instrument: two substantial facing page shapes, a precise central fold, and one restrained amber point suggesting the first principle from which understanding grows. Integrate a subtle capital P into the negative space only if it remains immediately clean and natural. Use two or three bold shapes rather than many fine lines.
Typography: refined contemporary editorial serif, similar in spirit to Fraunces, beautifully spaced; exact text "Principia Desk". No tagline.
Color palette: deep aubergine #141118 background, warm ivory #F2EDE4 lettering, amber #EF9F27 as the principal symbol color with a restrained muted violet #534AB7 secondary detail. This is the application's established palette.
Style: flat, vector-like precision, subtle rounded corners consistently throughout the mark, confident scholarly character, readable desktop identity. No photorealism or mockup scene. No shadows, gradients, texture, ornamental blueprint grids, tiny labels, dice, roulette wheels, graduation caps, brains, robots, generic AI sparkle stars, or stock atom symbols. One logo, not a sheet of competing variants.
```

## Refinement prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand, refinement of the attached Principia Desk logo candidate.
Keep the composition, spelling "Principia Desk", open-book symbol, warm ivory serif lettering and amber/violet palette.
Repair the image quality: redraw ALL symbol contours and letterforms cleanly with smooth antialiasing, solid unbroken fills, absolutely no speckles, torn edges, distressed texture, holes in strokes, yellow halos or clipping. The wordmark must look professionally typeset, refined and balanced, not excessively bold.
Render on a COMPLETELY OPAQUE, UNIFORM deep aubergine #141118 background across the entire rectangular image. This is a finished brand presentation, NOT a transparent cutout. Do not remove the background or apply transparency anywhere.
Simplify the central negative-space P so it reads as a deliberate, clean opening between the pages; keep the outer book silhouette and restrained violet point. Flat solid amber #EF9F27, muted violet #534AB7, ivory #F2EDE4. No gradients, shadows, shiny bevels, 3D, texture, extra text or decorations. Maintain generous margins and one horizontal logo lockup.
```
