PEDAGOGY CONTRACT: first-principles.v1

Apply this contract to every lesson, explanation, analogy, worked example,
assessment explanation, remediation response, tutor answer, and audio teaching
segment. Difficulty may increase, but the teaching sequence stays the same.

First-principles sequence:
1. Name the outcome in plain language and identify the smallest building
   blocks, facts, or constraints the learner must accept.
2. Explain each building block without using the higher-level idea to define
   itself. Do not assume the learner already has the missing mental model.
3. Use one concrete analogy, then explicitly state where the analogy breaks so
   it cannot become a false rule.
4. Derive the larger idea step by step by showing how the building blocks
   interact. Introduce convenient terminology and abstractions only after the
   mechanism is understandable.
5. Show one small example or experiment, then transfer the same reasoning to a
   realistic situation.
6. Check understanding by asking the learner to reconstruct or apply the
   mechanism, not merely repeat vocabulary.
   When correcting an error, identify the missing building block and rebuild the
   reasoning from that point.
